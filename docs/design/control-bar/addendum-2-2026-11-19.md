# Skreenrek control bar: addendum 2 (2026-11-19). Recording outline, Clear on the bar, Saved on the bar

Extends the control bar spec and 1.0.5 spec §4 (on-screen outline). Where they conflict, this wins.

## A. Recording outline (Raph, 09:16: "let's add a red outline when recording")

Replaces 1.0.5 §4 "never shows while recording".

- **Colour:** `NSColor.systemRed` (#FF3B30 light, #FF453A dark), the same red as the Stop button. Token `record-red`. Not a custom red.
- **Geometry:** identical to the pick outline: 3 pt stroke, just outside the window, corner radius = window radius + 3 (r19 on Tahoe, r13 on macOS 15). Same 0.5 pt rgba(0,0,0,.30) contrast edge; Increase Contrast makes it 1 pt rgba(0,0,0,.6). Only the colour changes, so nothing jumps when recording starts.
- **Motion:** solid, never pulses. The menu-bar item and the bar timer already say it's live; a pulsing frame around the user's work is noise.
  - Record starts (after any countdown): accent → red crossfade over 0.2 s.
  - Reduce Motion: colour swaps in one frame.
- **Visibility while recording:** shown the whole time, whether or not the panel is open. It follows moves and resizes within 100 ms, as today, and never takes clicks.
- **Saving:** recording is over, so the red goes away at once when saving starts.
  - Bar on (window stays picked, Raph 15:04 yesterday): outline goes back to accent (0.2 s crossfade, instant under Reduce Motion) and then follows the normal pick rules (visible while the panel or bar is showing it).
  - Bar off / pick dropped (including agent recordings of a user-picked window): outline fades out over 0.3 s (instant under Reduce Motion).
- **Region recordings:** yes, same red 3 pt frame drawn just outside the region rect, square corners (radius 0: a region has no window corners). Shown from record start to saving, same rules.
- **Full-screen recordings:** no frame. A red border on the display edge covers the user's own screen with no information the menu-bar item doesn't already give.
- **Hidden when:** "Show recording indicator" is off, or an agent recording passes `--no-hud`. Same rule as the bar: the frame is a recording indicator.
- **Capture:** the outline window keeps `sharingType = .none`. Acceptance: a real 10 s clip of a window and of a region shows no red at any frame, including the first and last.
- **Accessibility:** no VoiceOver element (decorative; the bar and menu-bar item carry state).

## B. Clear window on the bar (as built, PR #51)

Accepted as built. Recording it here as spec:

- `xmark` button at the trailing edge, 24×24 hit area, same hover 8%/5% and pressed 12%/9% tones as the panel icon buttons.
- Tooltip `Clear Window`, VoiceOver `Clear picked window` (same strings as the panel).
- Hidden while recording or saving. The bar keeps its width, so nothing reflows mid-recording.
- Bar is 336 pt wide (compact 228). Keep the 6 pt outer padding and 12 pt slot gaps from the original layout.
- Action = the panel's Clear: pick dropped, bar and outline go away at once.
- Outline-after-move bug fix: after a clip, the outline must redraw at the window's current frame, never its recording-start frame.

## Acceptance

1. Window pick → Record: outline turns red within 0.2 s of the first frame and stays solid.
2. Reduce Motion: no crossfade; colour swaps instantly.
3. Stop with bar on: outline back to accent immediately at saving; with bar off: fades out.
4. Region recording shows a square red frame; full-screen shows none.
5. Indicator off or `--no-hud`: no red frame.
6. No red pixels in a real clip (window and region).
7. Light and dark renders of: window recording, region recording, saving (bar on).

## C. Saved state on the bar (Raph, 09:17: "on save I think it should be the same record bar reused/tweaked rather than a new bar")

When the bar is showing at the moment a clip finishes saving, the bar itself turns into Saved. The separate Saved HUD doesn't appear. Same size, same surface and the same 6 pt padding. Nothing about the bar's frame moves.

### Layout, full bar (336 × 36)

| Area | x (pt) | Content |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Status (slots 1+2) | 6–198 | `checkmark.circle.fill` 16 pt `systemGreen` at x 14. `Saved · 0:07` 13 medium `labelColor` at x 36, with `.monospacedDigit()` on the duration. |
| | 146–170 | Icon button `folder`, 24×24, r6. Tooltip `Show in Finder`. |
| | 174–198 | Icon button `doc.on.doc`, 24×24, r6. Tooltip `Copy File Path`. |
| Slot 3 | 210–294 | **Record**, enabled. The window is still picked, so Raph can go again right away. |
| Clear | 306–330 | `xmark`, shown again. |

- The icon buttons use the same hover (8%/5%) and pressed (12%/9%) tones as the other icon buttons, with no cell fill at rest.
- After a click on Copy, the glyph turns into `checkmark` for 1.5 s and the tooltip and VoiceOver read `Copied`.
- Show in Finder reveals the file and ends Saved at once, so the bar goes back to Idle.

### Compact bar (228 × 36)

| Area | x (pt) | Content |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Slot 1 | 6–90 | Check 16 pt at x 14, then `Saved` 13 medium at x 36. The duration moves into the tooltip and VoiceOver. |
| Slot 3 | 102–186 | `folder` at x 116–140 and `doc.on.doc` at x 148–172, replacing Record while Saved shows. |
| Clear | 198–222 | `xmark` |

Record comes back when the bar returns to Idle.

### Timing

- Saved shows for **6 s**, then crossfades to Idle over 0.2 s.
- The countdown pauses while the pointer is over the bar and restarts at 6 s when the pointer leaves.
- Any of these ends Saved at once: clicking Record (a new recording starts), opening the Size pop-up, clicking Show in Finder, or clicking ×.
- Under Reduce Motion, every change is an instant swap.

### Saving to Saved

Nothing moves. The spinner crossfades into the green check, and `Saving clip…` crossfades into `Saved · 0:07`, over 0.2 s. The icon buttons, Record and × fade in over the same 0.2 s. Under Reduce Motion, it's one frame.

The on-screen outline is accent during Saved (§A, bar on).

### What × does

× means the same thing in every state: **Clear Window**. In Saved it drops the pick, and the bar and the outline go away at once. To dismiss Saved but keep the window, wait for the timeout or click Record.

### When there's no bar

- **Region clips, full-screen clips, and window clips with the bar off or hidden** (window minimised or on another Space): the existing solid Saved HUD shows as today, under `Show recording indicator`.
- **Agent recordings that drop the user's pick:** the bar goes away when saving starts. The HUD's Saved shows if the HUD is allowed.
- **`--no-hud`, or `Show recording indicator` off:** no Saved on the bar and no HUD. Same rule as the bar and the outline.

The rule in one line: if the bar is on screen when the save finishes, Saved shows on the bar. Otherwise the HUD handles it as today.

### Accessibility

- When Saved appears, post the announcement `Clip saved, 7 seconds`.
- The VoiceOver labels are `Show in Finder`, `Copy file path` and `Clear picked window`.
- The status area reads `Saved, 7 seconds`, plus the file name.

### Acceptance (adds to §A)

8. Stop a window clip with the bar on. The bar turns into Saved at the same frame, and no Saved HUD appears.
9. Saved returns to Idle after 6 s. Hovering pauses the countdown. Clicking Record from Saved starts a new clip.
10. Region and full-screen clips still show the HUD's Saved. `--no-hud` shows neither.
11. Light and dark renders of Saved at 336 and 228, plus Copied.
